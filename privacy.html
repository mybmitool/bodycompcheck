Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you. Period.

Last updated: 2026-06-14

1. Data Collection

BodyCompCheck does not collect, store, or transmit any personal health data you enter into our tools. All calculations run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Your body weight, measurements, and results never leave your device.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your tool inputs and calculation history for your convenience. This data is stored on your device only and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics (such as Plausible or similar) that do not use cookies, do not track individual users, and do not collect personal data. These tools collect only anonymized, aggregated traffic statistics.

4. Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense or affiliate networks. These services may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad preferences through Google Ads Settings.

5. Affiliate Links

Some articles contain affiliate links to products or services we recommend. If you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This does not influence our recommendations.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email privacy@bodycompcheck.com.
