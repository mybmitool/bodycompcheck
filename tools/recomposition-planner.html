How It Works

This planner calculates your maintenance calories using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation adjusted for activity level, then selects a strategy based on the gap between your current and goal body fat percentage. If the gap is large (>8%), it recommends a cut phase first. If moderate (0-8%), it recommends simultaneous recomposition. If you are already lean, it recommends a lean bulk. The tool then projects monthly milestones using realistic rates: body fat loss of 0.3-0.75% per month for recomposition, or 0.5-1.0% per month for dedicated cuts. Protein is set at 1.8g per kg of body weight to support muscle retention and growth. Remember: recomposition works best for beginners, returning lifters, and those with higher body fat. Advanced athletes may need dedicated bulk/cut cycles.
