The Accuracy Hierarchy

Every method has error. Know how much before you trust the number.

DEXA Scan

±1-2% error

Gold standard. Gives bone density, visceral fat, and regional breakdown.

Bod Pod

±2-3% error

Air displacement. No radiation. Fast and comfortable.

Skinfold Calipers

±3-5% error

Portable and affordable. Requires skill and consistency.

Consumer BIA Scale

±5-8% error

Convenient but hydration-dependent. Good for trends, not absolutes.

Navy Method

±4-8% error

Free and fast. Just a tape measure. Limited for athletes.

BMI

±10-15% error

The old shortcut. Can't see muscle. Often misclassifies active people.

Common Questions

Is my data stored on your servers?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your data is stored locally using localStorage.

Which method is most accurate?

DEXA is the gold standard with ±1-2% error. It also gives bone density and visceral fat data.

Can I use these tools instead of seeing a doctor?

No. Our tools are for educational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider.

Why does my body fat change day to day?

Hydration, food intake, and exercise timing affect readings. BIA scales can swing 3-5% day-to-day.
